# How-To Manual

Welcome to the WizTech Payroll how-to manual. Use the **Jump To** button in the **Know Your App** tab to move directly to any section.

## 1. Dashboard

### 1.1 Viewing the Dashboard
- Open the **Dashboard** from the sidebar.
- The Dashboard shows KPI cards for the selected period: active employees, gross payroll, total deductions, net payroll, PAYE, pension (employee and employer), FBT, and employer cost.
- Charts display payroll by department, monthly trend, headcount trend, and a department summary table.

### 1.2 Changing the Period
- Click the **Period** picker in the top-right of the Dashboard.
- Select the month you want to inspect.
- The page reloads with data for that period.

## 2. Employees

### 2.1 Adding a New Employee
- Open **Employees** from the sidebar.
- Click **Add Employee**.
- Fill in the form: employee ID, first name, last name, national ID, department, position, employment date, type, basic salary, salary frequency, allowances, bank name, account number, payment method, pension applicability, tax status, tax number, and notes.
- Click **Create** to save.

### 2.2 Editing an Employee
- In the Employees list, click the **Edit** icon for the employee you want to change.
- Update the fields as needed and click **Update**.

### 2.3 Deactivating an Employee
- Click the **Trash** icon next to an active employee to deactivate them.
- Confirm the action in the dialog. The employee is marked inactive but remains in the system.

## 3. Payroll

### 3.1 Running a Payroll Period
- Open **Payroll** from the sidebar.
- Select the period you want to run.
- Review the employee list and enter any overtime, allowances, or deductions.
- Click **Calculate** to preview the results.
- Click **Save** to commit the payroll for the period.

### 3.2 Generating Payslips
- After a payroll period is saved, open **Payslips**.
- Select the period and employee.
- The payslip shows gross pay, deductions, net pay, and statutory contributions.
- Use **Print / Save as PDF** to export the payslip.

## 4. Payslips

### 4.1 Viewing a Payslip
- Open **Payslips** from the sidebar.
- Choose a period and an employee from the dropdowns.
- The payslip displays automatically.

### 4.2 Exporting a Payslip as PDF
- On the payslip view, click **Print / Save as PDF**.
- In the print dialog, choose **Save as PDF** to download the file.

## 5. Reports

### 5.1 Running a Report
- Open **Reports** from the sidebar.
- Choose a report type (Payroll Register, Payroll Summary, Statutory Summary, Department Payroll, Bank Payment Schedule, or Employee Earnings History).
- Select a period and optionally a department.
- Click **Generate Report**.

### 5.2 Exporting CSV/Excel
- After generating a report, click **Export CSV** or **Export Excel** to download the data.

## 6. Settings

### 6.1 Updating Company Information
- Open **Settings** and select the **Company** tab.
- Edit the company name, address, phone, email, TPIN, and pension fund.
- Click **Save Company Settings**.

### 6.2 Managing PAYE Bands
- In the **Statutory** tab, scroll to the PAYE Tax Bands section.
- Add, edit, or remove tax bands. Each band has a from, to, and rate.
- The live preview shows the tax for sample incomes.
- Click **Save Statutory Settings** to apply.

### 6.3 Configuring Pension & FBT
- In the **Statutory** tab, update the employee and employer pension rates, maximum pensionable income, and pension fund name.
- In the **FBT Benefit Rules** section, add or edit fringe benefit rules.
- Click **Save Statutory Settings** to apply.

## 7. FBT

### 7.1 Understanding FBT
- Fringe Benefit Tax (FBT) is applied to certain non-cash benefits provided to employees, such as housing, motor vehicles, school fees, utilities, and airtime.
- FBT rules are configured in the **Settings → Statutory → FBT Benefit Rules** section.

### 7.2 Adding an FBT Rule
- In the **FBT Benefit Rules** section, click **Add FBT Rule**.
- Fill in the benefit type, version, effective dates, rule type, classification, and value.
- Save the rule. It will be applied automatically in the next payroll run.

### 7.3 Reviewing FBT on Payroll and Payslips
- After a payroll run, the FBT amount appears in the payroll register and on employee payslips.
- Use the **Reports** tab to generate a Statutory Summary for compliance reporting.
